CMAL key and exit guide

Where the key and the exit can be on each Challenge Mode floor, with maps for the floors whose layout never changes (apart from which way it's turned). Percentages are from thousands of generated layouts per floor.

Rules for every floor

  • KeyAn enemy carrying the key spawns only on its own kind of spot (and, unless it's a plant-spot enemy, never right by the pod). The maps mark those spots in gold.
  • ExitSome enemies never spawn near the exit. On the maps, one standing on a red circle rules out the exit spot joined to it. Each floor says which enemies count. It works both ways: once you've found the exit, the key carrier isn't on a red circle joined to it.
  • KeyA loose key (no enemy carries it) is usually in an alcove: over 90% on most floors. It's almost never in an alcove off the pod's room when the floor has alcoves elsewhere (Rumbling Grotto 1 is the exception).
  • ExitThe exit's alcove never holds anything else. Far alcoves are slightly likelier than ones off the pod's room.
  • KeyExitGates go in front of alcoves that have something in them first. A gated alcove is almost never empty.
  • ExitFor the exit's exact odds on a known layout, tick Hole odds in CaveGen3.

Reading the maps

Each percentage is the chance for that spot, given where the pod is. Floors where the pod can start in more than one place get a map for each. The floor can appear turned in game; the map shows one way round.
